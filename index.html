About Us

Bringing Solar Energy to Every Rooftop in Kochi

KochiSolar has been at the forefront of Kerala's renewable energy revolution since 2010. Based in the heart of Kochi, we are committed to making clean, affordable solar energy accessible to every home and business in the region.

As a MNRE-approved channel partner and empanelled with KSEB for net metering, we provide end-to-end solar solutions with the highest quality panels, inverters, and installation practices.
